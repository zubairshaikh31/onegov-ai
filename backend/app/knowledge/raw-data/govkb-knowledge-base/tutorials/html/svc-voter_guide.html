Voter ID / Electoral Roll Enrolment (Form 6) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://voterportal.eci.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Registration of names in the electoral roll and issuance of Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC / Voter ID) by the Election Commission of India.

Eligibility

Indian citizen, 18+ years on qualifying date, ordinary resident of the constituency.

Benefits

Right to vote in elections; EPIC serves as identity and address proof.

Documents

  • Age proof (birth certificate / class 10 cert) (Mandatory)
  • Address proof (Mandatory)
  • Passport-size photo (online) (Mandatory)
  • Declaration of ordinary residence (Mandatory)
  • Aadhaar (for identity linkage, voluntary) (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit nvsp.in or voterportal.eci.gov.in and register/login.
  2. Step 2: Fill Form 6 (new enrolment) with details and upload documents.
  3. Step 3: Submit and note reference number.
  4. Step 4: BLO verifies at residence; name added to draft roll.
  5. Step 5: Download e-EPIC once approved.

Fees

Free.

Timeline

Typically 2-4 weeks after verification; before each summary revision.

FAQs

Q: Can NRI citizens enrol?
A: Yes, under Form 6A if aged 18+ and not enrolled in India.

Q: Is Aadhaar mandatory for voter enrolment?
A: No, Aadhaar is voluntary for identity; other documents are accepted.
