Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://abc.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

National digital storehouse of academic credits earned from HEIs, enabling transfer and accumulation.

Eligibility

Students of participating Higher Education Institutions.

Benefits

Store credits, transfer across institutions, flexible degree pathways.

Documents

  • Aadhaar / ABC ID (Mandatory)
  • Student HEI enrollment (Mandatory)
  • Previous credit records (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Create ABC ID via abc.gov.in / institutional link.
  2. Step 2: HEI deposits earned credits.
  3. Step 3: View and manage credit stack.
  4. Step 4: Transfer/redeem per framework.

Fees

Free.

Timeline

Credits deposited per academic cycle.
