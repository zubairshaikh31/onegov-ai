# Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.pmjdy.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
National mission for financial inclusion providing zero-balance bank accounts with RuPay debit card and insurance.

## Eligibility
Any Indian citizen (resident) above 10 years; one account per person.

## Benefits
Zero-balance account, RuPay card, accident insurance, overdraft, DBT access.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Identity proof (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Visit bank/Bank Mitra with Aadhaar and proof.
2. Open account; get RuPay card.
3. Avail DBT and insurance benefits.
