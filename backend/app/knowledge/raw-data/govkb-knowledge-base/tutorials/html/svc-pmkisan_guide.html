PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://pmkisan.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Direct income support of Rs. 6,000/year to eligible farmer families, paid in three equal instalments of Rs. 2,000 directly into bank accounts.

Eligibility

Small and marginal farmer families (cultivable landholding) as per land records; excludes institutional landholders, higher-income categories.

Benefits

Rs. 6,000 per year direct transfer; supports farm input costs.

Documents

  • Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Bank account (DBT-linked) (Mandatory)
  • Landholding records / Khatauni (Mandatory)
  • Samagra / state ID where integrated (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit pmkisan.gov.in -> 'New Farmer Registration'.
  2. Step 2: Enter Aadhaar and land/account details.
  3. Step 3: Verify and submit; note registration number.
  4. Step 4: District/State validates land records.
  5. Step 5: Instalments credited to bank account; check status online.

Fees

Free.

Timeline

Instalments released periodically; first credit after verification (weeks).

FAQs

Q: Why is my instalment pending?
A: Commonly due to Aadhaar-bank seeding mismatch or pending e-KYC; complete e-KYC on the portal.

Q: Are tenant farmers eligible?
A: Benefit is linked to landholding records in the owner's name; tenant farmers are generally not separate beneficiaries.
