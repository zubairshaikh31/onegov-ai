# DigiLocker — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.digilocker.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Digital locker to store and share verified electronic documents issued by government agencies; reduce physical paperwork.

## Eligibility
Indian citizens with Aadhaar (used for sign-up).

## Benefits
Instant access to issued documents (marksheets, licences), verified e-documents, paperless verification.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (for sign-up/authentication) (Mandatory)
- Mobile number (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Sign up at digilocker.gov.in with Aadhaar OTP.
2. Link mobile; set username/password.
3. Fetch issued documents from registered issuers.
4. Upload personal documents; share via URI/QR.

## Fees
Free.

## Timeline
Instant; document fetch depends on issuer.
