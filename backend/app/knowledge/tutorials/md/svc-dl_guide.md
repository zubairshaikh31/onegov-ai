# Driving Licence (Sarathi / Parivahan) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://parivahan.gov.in. Last updated 2025-01-01._

## Introduction
Application for learner's licence, driving licence, and renewals through the Parivahan Sarathi portal.

## Eligibility
Minimum age 16 (gearless <50cc) / 18 (LMV); pass driving test.

## Benefits
Legal authorisation to drive; valid identity/address proof.

## Documents
- Proof of Age (Mandatory)
- Proof of Address (Mandatory)
- Passport photos (Mandatory)
- Medical certificate (Form 1A for certain categories) (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Visit Sarathi Parivahan and select your state.
2. Apply for Learner's Licence and pay fees.
3. Take the online learner's test.
4. After the mandatory period, apply for the driving licence.
5. Book and pass the driving test at the RTO.
6. Receive the DL by post / download.

## Fees
Learner's ~Rs.150; DL ~Rs.200+ (varies by state RTO).

## Timeline
Learner's licence same day (online test); DL after driving test, ~7-30 days.
