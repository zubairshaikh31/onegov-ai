PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://pmkisan.gov.in.

Eligibility

Small and marginal farmer families (cultivable landholding) as per land records; excludes institutional landholders, higher-income categories.

Benefits

Rs. 6,000 per year direct transfer; supports farm input costs.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account (DBT-linked)
  • Landholding records / Khatauni
  • Samagra / state ID where integrated

Process

  1. Visit pmkisan.gov.in -> 'New Farmer Registration'.
  2. Enter Aadhaar and land/account details.
  3. Verify and submit; note registration number.
  4. District/State validates land records.
  5. Instalments credited to bank account; check status online.

Official: https://pmkisan.gov.in
