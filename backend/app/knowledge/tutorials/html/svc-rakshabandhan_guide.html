Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.mygov.in.

Eligibility

Indian citizens aged 18-70 with a linked bank account.

Benefits

Low-cost accidental death/disablement cover; auto-debit enrolment.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account

Process

  1. Visit bank or jansuraksha.gov.in.
  2. Give consent for auto-debit of Rs. 20.
  3. Get enrolled; download certificate.

Official: https://www.mygov.in
