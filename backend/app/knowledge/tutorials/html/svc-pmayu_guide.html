Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://pmaymis.gov.in.

Eligibility

Urban households in EWS/LIG/MIG categories with no pucca house; income and property criteria apply.

Benefits

Interest subsidy on home loan (CLSS) and support for house construction.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Income proof
  • Address proof
  • Bank account
  • Property/land documents
  • Caste/category certificate

Process

  1. Visit pmaymis.gov.in; check eligibility.
  2. Apply under beneficiary-led / CLSS component.
  3. Enter Aadhaar, income, bank, property details.
  4. Verification by ULB/state.
  5. Subsidy/assistance as per component.

Official: https://pmaymis.gov.in
