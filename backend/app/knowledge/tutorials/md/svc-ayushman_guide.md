# Ayushman Bharat Health & Wellness Centres — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://abdm.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Network of Health & Wellness Centres delivering comprehensive primary health care at the community level.

## Eligibility
All residents; ABHA-linked services; PM-JAY for eligible families.

## Benefits
Free essential health services, screenings, and referrals at HWCs.

## Documents
- ABHA ID (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Ration/ID proof (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Locate nearest Health & Wellness Centre.
2. Register with ABHA/Aadhaar.
3. Avail free primary care services.
