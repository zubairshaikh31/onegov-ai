# Maharashtra Chief Minister Relief Fund — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.maharashtra.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
State relief fund assisting citizens in distress, natural calamities, and medical emergencies.

## Eligibility
Maharashtra residents in notified distress/calamity situations.

## Benefits
Financial assistance for calamity, medical, and ex-gratia support.

## Documents
- Identity proof (Mandatory)
- Distress/calamity proof (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Apply through district administration / online portal.
2. Verification of distress.
3. Disbursement of relief.
