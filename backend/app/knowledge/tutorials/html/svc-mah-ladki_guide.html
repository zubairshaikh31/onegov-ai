Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in.

Eligibility

Women aged 21-65 residing in Maharashtra, meeting income and other criteria as notified.

Benefits

Monthly financial assistance credited via DBT to eligible women.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Domicile certificate
  • Income certificate
  • Bank account (Aadhaar linked)
  • Ration card
  • Marriage certificate (if domicile via spouse)

Process

  1. Register on the Ladki Bahin portal or Narishakti Doot app.
  2. Enter Aadhaar and personal details.
  3. Upload eligibility documents.
  4. Submit and note the application number.
  5. Track approval and DBT credit status.

Official: https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in
