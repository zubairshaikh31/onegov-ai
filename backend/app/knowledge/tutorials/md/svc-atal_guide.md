# Atal Pension Yojana — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Guaranteed minimum pension of Rs.1000-5000 for unorganised workers.

## Eligibility
Adults 18-40 in unorganised sector.

## Benefits
Fixed pension after 60.

## Documents
- Aadhaar
- Bank

## Process
1. Enroll via bank/PO.
2. Auto-debit contribution.
