Startup India Recognition — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://www.startupindia.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

DPIIT recognition of startups to avail tax benefits, easier compliance, IPR fast-track, and funding access.

Eligibility

Entity incorporated as Pvt Ltd/LLP/Partnership, <=10 years old, turnover <= Rs. 100 crore, working toward innovation.

Benefits

Tax exemption (80-IAC/Section 56), faster patents, self-certification, government tenders, Fund of Funds.

Documents

  • Incorporation certificate (Mandatory)
  • PAN (Mandatory)
  • Description of innovation (Mandatory)
  • Director/Promoter details (Mandatory)
  • Pitch deck (Optional)
  • IPR details (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Incorporate the entity.
  2. Step 2: Register on startupindia.gov.in.
  3. Step 3: Apply for DPIIT recognition with details.
  4. Step 4: Self-certify and submit; get recognition number.
  5. Step 5: Avail benefits (tax/patent/tender).

Fees

Free for recognition.

Timeline

Recognition typically within days of submission.
