West Bengal Duare Sarkar / e-District (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://edistrict.wb.gov.in.

Eligibility

West Bengal residents per service criteria.

Benefits

Doorstep certificate and scheme enrolment camps.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income proof

Process

  1. Attend Duare Sarkar camp or apply online.
  2. Submit documents.
  3. Receive service.

Official: https://edistrict.wb.gov.in
