# UMANG (Unified Mobile App for New-age Governance) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://umang.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Single mobile app aggregating central, state, and utility services (EPFO, income tax, passport, bill pay, etc.).

## Eligibility
Any citizen with a smartphone; services may need respective IDs.

## Benefits
One app for many government services; multilingual; Aadhaar/PAN-based auth.

## Documents
- Mobile number (Mandatory)
- Service-specific IDs (Aadhaar/PAN/UAN) (Mandatory)
- Bank details for payments (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Download UMANG app / visit web.umang.gov.in.
2. Register with mobile OTP.
3. Browse departments and services.
4. Authenticate per service and transact.

## Fees
Free app; service charges as applicable.

## Timeline
Per service.
