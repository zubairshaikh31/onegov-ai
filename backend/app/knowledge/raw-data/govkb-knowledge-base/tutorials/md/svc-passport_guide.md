# Passport Issuance (Passport Seva) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://passportindia.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Issuance/renewal of Indian passports (ordinary, Tatkaal, diplomatic/official) by the Ministry of External Affairs through Passport Seva Kendras (PSK) and Post Office PSKs.

## Eligibility

Indian citizen; must provide proof of date of birth, address, and nationality. Minors require parent consent.

## Benefits

Internationally recognised travel document; enables visa-free/visa-on-arrival access; Tatkaal for urgent issue.

## Documents Required

- **Proof of Date of Birth** (Mandatory)
- **Proof of Address (current)** (Mandatory)
- **Proof of nationality / old passport (if renewing)** (Mandatory)
- **Annexure as applicable** (Mandatory)
- **Marriage certificate (name change)** (Optional)
- **ECNR eligibility proof** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Register on passportindia.gov.in and fill the application form.
**Step 2:** Pay fee and book appointment at nearest PSK/PopSK.
**Step 3:** Visit PSK with originals + photocopies for document verification and biometric capture.
**Step 4:** Police verification (for fresh passports) at registered address.
**Step 5:** Passport printed and dispatched; track status online.

## Fees

Normal 36-page: Rs. 1500. Tatkaal 36-page: Rs. 3500. (10-year validity for adults; minors 5 years / till age 18).

## Timeline

Tatkaal: ~1-3 days post verification. Normal: ~30 days (post police verification).

## FAQs

**Q: Can I apply from a different state than my address?**
A: You must apply from your current address jurisdiction; police verification happens at the address given.

**Q: What is the difference between Normal and Tatkaal?**
A: Tatkaal is an expedited service with higher fees and pre-verification; Normal follows standard police verification.
