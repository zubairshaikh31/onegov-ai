Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.pmjdy.gov.in.

Eligibility

Any Indian citizen (resident) above 10 years; one account per person.

Benefits

Zero-balance account, RuPay card, accident insurance, overdraft, DBT access.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Identity proof
  • Address proof

Process

  1. Visit bank/Bank Mitra with Aadhaar and proof.
  2. Open account; get RuPay card.
  3. Avail DBT and insurance benefits.

Official: https://www.pmjdy.gov.in
