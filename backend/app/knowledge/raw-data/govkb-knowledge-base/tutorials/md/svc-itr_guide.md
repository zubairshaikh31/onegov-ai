# Income Tax e-Filing & Return (ITR) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://www.incometax.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Online filing of Income Tax Returns, PAN services, and tax payments by the Income Tax Department.

## Eligibility

Individuals/entities with taxable income or mandated filing (e.g., refund, foreign asset, high turnover).

## Benefits

Legal compliance, refund claims, proof of income, easier loans/visa.

## Documents Required

- **PAN** (Mandatory)
- **Aadhaar (linked)** (Mandatory)
- **Form 16 / 16A** (Mandatory)
- **Bank statements** (Mandatory)
- **Investment proofs** (Mandatory)
- **Capital gains statements** (Optional)
- **Donation receipts** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Register/login on incometax.gov.in.
**Step 2:** Choose correct ITR form.
**Step 3:** Pre-fill and enter income/deductions.
**Step 4:** Validate, pay tax (if any), submit.
**Step 5:** Verify via Aadhaar OTP / EVC / send ITR-V.

## Fees

No government filing fee; late fees under Section 234F if delayed.

## Timeline

Filing instant; refund within months of processing.
