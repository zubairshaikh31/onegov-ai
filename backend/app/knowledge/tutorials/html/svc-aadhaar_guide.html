Aadhaar Enrolment & Update (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://uidai.gov.in.

Eligibility

Any resident of India (including infants) irrespective of citizenship status of parents for enrolment; updates available to existing holders.

Benefits

Single portable ID accepted nationwide; enables DBT, subsidy linkage, KYC, and access to many government schemes.

Documents

  • Proof of Identity (PoI)
  • Proof of Address (PoA)
  • Date of Birth proof
  • Introducer / Head of Family based enrolment where documents unavailable

Process

  1. Locate nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre / Aadhaar Seva Kendra.
  2. Fill enrolment/update form and submit supporting documents.
  3. Provide biometric (fingerprints, iris) and photograph.
  4. Collect acknowledgement slip with Enrolment ID (EID).
  5. Download e-Aadhaar once UID is generated (verify on uidai.gov.in).

Official: https://uidai.gov.in
