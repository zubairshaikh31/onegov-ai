SWAYAM (Free Online Courses) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://swayam.gov.in.

Eligibility

Open to all learners; some courses have eligibility for credit/ exam.

Benefits

Free quality courses, certificates (with exam fee), academic credit transfer where recognised.

Documents

  • Email/mobile for sign-up
  • Institution enrollment for credit

Process

  1. Sign up on swayam.gov.in.
  2. Browse and enrol in courses.
  3. Attend video lectures, assignments.
  4. Take proctored exam (optional, paid) for certificate.

Official: https://swayam.gov.in
