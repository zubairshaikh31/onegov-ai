# Domicile / Residence Certificate (Maharashtra) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in. Last updated 2025-01-01._

## Introduction
Certificate confirming that the applicant is a permanent resident of Maharashtra, issued via Aaple Sarkar.

## Eligibility
Persons residing in Maharashtra for the qualifying period as per rules.

## Benefits
Required for education admissions, government jobs and domicile-based benefits.

## Documents
- Aadhaar / ID proof (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Proof of residence period (Mandatory)
- School leaving certificate (Mandatory)
- Birth certificate (Optional)
- Affidavit (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Login to Aaple Sarkar portal.
2. Select Revenue Department > Age Nationality and Domicile Certificate.
3. Fill the form and upload documents.
4. Pay fees and submit.
5. Download the digitally signed certificate.

## Fees
Nominal (approx Rs.33-40).

## Timeline
15 working days (RTS standard).
