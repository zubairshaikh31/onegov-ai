e-Shram (National Database of Unorganised Workers) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://eshram.gov.in.

Eligibility

Unorganised workers aged 16-59, not members of EPFO/ESIC, with Aadhaar.

Benefits

e-Shram card; eligibility for future social security/welfare schemes; portability.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar
  • Occupation / skill details

Process

  1. Visit eshram.gov.in; self-register with Aadhaar OTP.
  2. Fill personal, occupation, and bank details.
  3. Submit; download e-Shram card (UAN).
  4. Update details as needed.

Official: https://eshram.gov.in
