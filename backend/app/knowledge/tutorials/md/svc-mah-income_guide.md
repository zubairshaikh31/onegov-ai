# Income Certificate (Maharashtra) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in. Last updated 2025-01-01._

## Introduction
Certificate stating the annual income of a family, issued through Aaple Sarkar / MahaOnline for various welfare and scholarship purposes.

## Eligibility
Residents of Maharashtra requiring proof of family income.

## Benefits
Required for scholarships, fee concessions, reservations and welfare schemes.

## Documents
- Aadhaar / ID proof (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Income proof (salary slip/self-declaration) (Mandatory)
- Ration card (Mandatory)
- Talathi report (Optional)
- Affidavit (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Register/login on Aaple Sarkar (RTS) portal.
2. Select Revenue Department > Income Certificate.
3. Fill applicant details and upload documents.
4. Pay the fee online.
5. Track application; download the digitally signed certificate.

## Fees
Nominal (approx Rs.33-40 via Aaple Sarkar).

## Timeline
15 working days (as per RTS Act service standard).
