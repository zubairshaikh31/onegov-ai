Aaple Sarkar / MahaOnline Citizen Services (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in.

Eligibility

Residents of Maharashtra; service-specific criteria.

Benefits

Single window for caste/income/residence certificates, scheme applications, grievance redressal.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Service-specific documents
  • Earlier certificates

Process

  1. Register on Aaple Sarkar portal.
  2. Select department/service (certificate/scheme).
  3. Fill form and upload documents.
  4. Submit; track application.
  5. Collect certificate (online download / SETU centre).

Official: https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
