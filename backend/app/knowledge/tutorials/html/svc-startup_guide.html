Startup India Recognition (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.startupindia.gov.in.

Eligibility

Entity incorporated as Pvt Ltd/LLP/Partnership, <=10 years old, turnover <= Rs. 100 crore, working toward innovation.

Benefits

Tax exemption (80-IAC/Section 56), faster patents, self-certification, government tenders, Fund of Funds.

Documents

  • Incorporation certificate
  • PAN
  • Description of innovation
  • Director/Promoter details
  • Pitch deck
  • IPR details

Process

  1. Incorporate the entity.
  2. Register on startupindia.gov.in.
  3. Apply for DPIIT recognition with details.
  4. Self-certify and submit; get recognition number.
  5. Avail benefits (tax/patent/tender).

Official: https://www.startupindia.gov.in
