# Mukhyamantri Majhi Ladki Bahin Yojana — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://ladakibahin.maharashtra.gov.in. Last updated 2025-01-01._

## Introduction
State scheme providing monthly financial assistance to eligible women residents of Maharashtra.

## Eligibility
Women aged 21-65 residing in Maharashtra, meeting income and other criteria as notified.

## Benefits
Monthly financial assistance credited via DBT to eligible women.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Domicile certificate (Mandatory)
- Income certificate (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Aadhaar linked) (Mandatory)
- Ration card (Mandatory)
- Marriage certificate (if domicile via spouse) (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Register on the Ladki Bahin portal or Narishakti Doot app.
2. Enter Aadhaar and personal details.
3. Upload eligibility documents.
4. Submit and note the application number.
5. Track approval and DBT credit status.

## Fees
Free.

## Timeline
Verification-dependent; benefits via DBT after approval.
