National Apprenticeship (Apprenticeship India) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://apprenticeshipindia.gov.in.

Eligibility

ITI/diploma/degree holders (aged 14+ for certain categories) per Apprentices Act.

Benefits

Stipend during training; industry experience; certification; employment pathway.

Documents

  • Educational certificates
  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account
  • Category certificate (if applicable)

Process

  1. Register on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in as candidate.
  2. Search establishments and apply.
  3. Sign contract; undergo training with stipend.

Official: https://apprenticeshipindia.gov.in
