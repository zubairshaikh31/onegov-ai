> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.indiapost.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Postal, parcel, Speed Post, savings accounts, POSB, IPPB banking, and government agency services.

## Eligibility
Open to all citizens; accounts per India Post schemes.

## Benefits
Nationwide postal + financial inclusion; IPPB digital banking; agency services.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Identity proof (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Passport-size photo (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Visit nearest post office or indiapost.gov.in.
2. Open account / avail service with KYC.
3. Use IPPB app for digital banking.
