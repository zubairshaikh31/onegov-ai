# MahaDBT (Maharashtra Scholarship & DBT) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Maharashtra's integrated DBT portal for state scholarships and benefits to eligible students/citizens.

## Eligibility

Maharashtra residents meeting scheme criteria (caste/income/merit).

## Benefits

Direct benefit transfer of scholarships and schemes to bank account; single window.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar** (Mandatory)
- **Bank account** (Mandatory)
- **Income/caste certificate** (Mandatory)
- **Marksheets** (Mandatory)
- **Disability/category certificates** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Register on MahaDBT portal.
**Step 2:** Complete profile with Aadhaar/bank/certificates.
**Step 3:** Select eligible schemes; apply.
**Step 4:** Institute/DC verification.
**Step 5:** DBT to bank account.

## Fees

Free.

## Timeline

Per scheme cycle.
