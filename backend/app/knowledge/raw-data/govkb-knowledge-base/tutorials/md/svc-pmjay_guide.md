# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://pmjay.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

World's largest health assurance scheme providing Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization to eligible poor and vulnerable families.

## Eligibility

Based on SECC deprivation criteria and socio-economic parameters; beneficiaries identified via NHA database. States may expand.

## Benefits

Cashless treatment at empanelled public/private hospitals; no cap on family size/age.

## Documents Required

- **Ayushman card / ABHA** (Mandatory)
- **Eligibility confirmation (SECC/Rural-Urban)** (Mandatory)
- **ID proof (Aadhaar/Voter/ration)** (Mandatory)
- **PMJAY e-card print** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Check eligibility via pmjay.gov.in 'Am I Eligible'.
**Step 2:** Visit Ayushman Bharat / CSC kendra with ID.
**Step 3:** Create Ayushman card / e-card.
**Step 4:** Visit empanelled hospital; show card at helpdesk.
**Step 5:** Cashless admission and treatment; claim settled directly.

## Fees

Free for eligible beneficiaries (no premium).

## Timeline

Card creation same day; treatment cashless at empanelled hospitals.
