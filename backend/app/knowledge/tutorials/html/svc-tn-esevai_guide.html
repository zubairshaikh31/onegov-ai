Tamil Nadu e-Sevai / e-District (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://eservices.tn.gov.in.

Eligibility

Tamil Nadu residents per service criteria.

Benefits

Online/offline certificates and service delivery.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income proof

Process

  1. Visit e-Sevai centre or portal.
  2. Apply; upload docs.
  3. Collect certificate.

Official: https://eservices.tn.gov.in
