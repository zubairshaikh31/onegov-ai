# CoWIN (Vaccination) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.cowin.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Platform for COVID vaccination registration and certificates.

## Eligibility
All eligible citizens.

## Benefits
Vaccination slots and certificate.

## Documents
- Aadhaar/ID

## Process
1. Register on CoWIN.
2. Book slot.
3. Get certificate.
