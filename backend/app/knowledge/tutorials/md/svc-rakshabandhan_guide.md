# Pradhan Mantri Suraksha Bima Yojana (PMSBY) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.mygov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Government accident insurance scheme with Rs. 2 lakh cover for death/disablement at premium Rs. 20/year.

## Eligibility
Indian citizens aged 18-70 with a linked bank account.

## Benefits
Low-cost accidental death/disablement cover; auto-debit enrolment.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Visit bank or jansuraksha.gov.in.
2. Give consent for auto-debit of Rs. 20.
3. Get enrolled; download certificate.
