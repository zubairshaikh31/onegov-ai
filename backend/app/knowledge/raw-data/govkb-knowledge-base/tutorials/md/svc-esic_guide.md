# ESIC (Employees' State Insurance) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://www.esic.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Social security and health insurance scheme for workers in covered establishments (medical, sickness, maternity, disability benefits).

## Eligibility

Employees with wage up to Rs. 21,000/month (Rs. 25,000 for persons with disability) in ESIC-covered factories/establishments.

## Benefits

Cashless medical care for worker and family, sickness/maternity benefits, disability and dependent benefits.

## Documents Required

- **IP number / UAN** (Mandatory)
- **ESIC card / Pehchan card** (Mandatory)
- **Aadhaar** (Mandatory)
- **Bank account** (Mandatory)
- **Employer certificate** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Employer registers establishment and enrols employees (Form 1).
**Step 2:** Employee gets Insurance/Pehchan card.
**Step 3:** Avail treatment at ESIC dispensary/hospital with card.
**Step 4:** File claims (sickness/maternity) online if applicable.

## Fees

Contribution by employer (3.25%) and employee (0.75%) on wages.

## Timeline

Enrolment by employer; benefits as per claim processing.
