Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABHA) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://abdm.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Creates a digital health ecosystem: ABHA ID, Health ID, Personal Health Records (PHR), and interoperable health information exchange.

Eligibility

Any Indian resident (voluntary opt-in).

Benefits

Single health ID to link records across providers; consent-based sharing; easier continuity of care.

Documents

  • Aadhaar / Driving Licence / any govt ID for ABHA creation (Mandatory)
  • Mobile number for OTP (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit abdm.gov.in or ABHA app.
  2. Step 2: Authenticate with Aadhaar OTP or other ID.
  3. Step 3: Create 14-digit ABHA number / Health ID.
  4. Step 4: Link PHR address and records from providers.
  5. Step 5: Manage consent for record sharing.

Fees

Free.

Timeline

Instant ABHA creation.
