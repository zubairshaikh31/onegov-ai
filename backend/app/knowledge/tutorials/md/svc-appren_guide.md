# National Apprenticeship (Apprenticeship India) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://apprenticeshipindia.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
National Apprenticeship Training scheme connecting apprentices with establishments for paid on-the-job training.

## Eligibility
ITI/diploma/degree holders (aged 14+ for certain categories) per Apprentices Act.

## Benefits
Stipend during training; industry experience; certification; employment pathway.

## Documents
- Educational certificates (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)
- Category certificate (if applicable) (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Register on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in as candidate.
2. Search establishments and apply.
3. Sign contract; undergo training with stipend.
