# Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (LPG) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.pmuy.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Provides deposit-free LPG connections to women from below-poverty-line households.

## Eligibility
Women (18+) from BPL households as per SECC criteria.

## Benefits
Deposit-free LPG connection; cheaper cooking fuel; health benefits.

## Documents
- BPL card / SECC details (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Visit pmuy.gov.in or nearest LPG distributor.
2. Submit KYC with Aadhaar/bank/BPL proof.
3. Get deposit-free connection and cylinder.
