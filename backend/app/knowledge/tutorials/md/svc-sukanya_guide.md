# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.indiapost.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Small-savings scheme for girl children (under 10 at opening) with attractive tax-free returns.

## Eligibility
Parents/guardians of a girl child below age 10; one account per child (max 2 per family).

## Benefits
High interest (govt-set), tax-free, partial withdrawal for education/marriage at 18+.

## Documents
- Birth certificate of girl child (Mandatory)
- Parent guardian ID (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Photograph (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Open at post office or authorised bank.
2. Deposit yearly (min Rs. 250).
3. Manage until maturity at age 21.
