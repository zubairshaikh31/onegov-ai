# Mahabhulekh (7/12 & 8A Land Records) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Maharashtra land record portal to view and download 7/12 (Satbara) and 8A extracts online.

## Eligibility
Landowners / general public for non-certified view; certified copies per process.

## Benefits
Instant access to land ownership, tenure, and mutation status; supports loans and transactions.

## Documents
- Survey number / village / district details (Mandatory)
- Owner name (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Visit mahabhulekh.maharashtra.gov.in.
2. Select district and 7/12 (Satbara) or 8A.
3. Enter village/survey/Gat number.
4. View and download extract (print).

## Fees
Free view; nominal fee for certified copies.

## Timeline
Instant view; certified copy per taluka office process.
