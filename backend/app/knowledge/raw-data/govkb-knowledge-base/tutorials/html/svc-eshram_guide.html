e-Shram (National Database of Unorganised Workers) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://eshram.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

National database and registration of unorganised sector workers to extend social security benefits.

Eligibility

Unorganised workers aged 16-59, not members of EPFO/ESIC, with Aadhaar.

Benefits

e-Shram card; eligibility for future social security/welfare schemes; portability.

Documents

  • Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Bank account (Mandatory)
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Occupation / skill details (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit eshram.gov.in; self-register with Aadhaar OTP.
  2. Step 2: Fill personal, occupation, and bank details.
  3. Step 3: Submit; download e-Shram card (UAN).
  4. Step 4: Update details as needed.

Fees

Free.

Timeline

Instant card generation after Aadhaar OTP verification.
