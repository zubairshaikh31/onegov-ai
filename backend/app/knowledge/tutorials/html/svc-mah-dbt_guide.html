MahaDBT (Maharashtra Scholarship & DBT) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in.

Eligibility

Maharashtra residents meeting scheme criteria (caste/income/merit).

Benefits

Direct benefit transfer of scholarships and schemes to bank account; single window.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account
  • Income/caste certificate
  • Marksheets
  • Disability/category certificates

Process

  1. Register on MahaDBT portal.
  2. Complete profile with Aadhaar/bank/certificates.
  3. Select eligible schemes; apply.
  4. Institute/DC verification.
  5. DBT to bank account.

Official: https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in
