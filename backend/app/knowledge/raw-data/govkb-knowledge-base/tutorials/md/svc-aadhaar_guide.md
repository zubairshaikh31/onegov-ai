# Aadhaar Enrolment & Update — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://uidai.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

12-digit unique identity number issued by UIDAI to residents of India, based on demographic and biometric data. Used as proof of identity and address and as a KYC token across government and private services.

## Eligibility

Any resident of India (including infants) irrespective of citizenship status of parents for enrolment; updates available to existing holders.

## Benefits

Single portable ID accepted nationwide; enables DBT, subsidy linkage, KYC, and access to many government schemes.

## Documents Required

- **Proof of Identity (PoI)** (Mandatory)
- **Proof of Address (PoA)** (Mandatory)
- **Date of Birth proof** (Mandatory)
- **Introducer / Head of Family based enrolment where documents unavailable** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Locate nearest Aadhaar enrolment centre / Aadhaar Seva Kendra.
**Step 2:** Fill enrolment/update form and submit supporting documents.
**Step 3:** Provide biometric (fingerprints, iris) and photograph.
**Step 4:** Collect acknowledgement slip with Enrolment ID (EID).
**Step 5:** Download e-Aadhaar once UID is generated (verify on uidai.gov.in).

## Fees

Enrolment: free. Demographic update: free. Biometric update: Rs. 100 (inclusive of GST) as per UIDAI fee schedule.

## Timeline

Enrolment: up to 90 days for UID generation (typically sooner). Update: up to 30 days.
