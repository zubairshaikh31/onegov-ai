# MGNREGA (Rural Employment) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://nrega.nic.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Mahatma Gandhi NREGA guarantees 100 days wage employment to rural households.

## Eligibility
Rural households willing to do unskilled manual work.

## Benefits
Guaranteed 100 days employment, wage.

## Documents
- Job card (issued on demand)
- Aadhaar

## Process
1. Demand work at gram panchayat.
2. Get job card.
3. Work and get wages.
