Maharashtra Farmer Welfare Schemes (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://mahaagri.gov.in.

Eligibility

Maharashtra farmers with land records; scheme-specific criteria.

Benefits

Input subsidies, loan waiver/relief, crop insurance, drip/solar subsidies.

Documents

  • 7/12 land record
  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account
  • Caste/category cert (if applicable)

Process

  1. Visit mahaagri.gov.in.
  2. Select scheme; apply with land/identity docs.
  3. Verification; benefit disbursal.

Official: https://mahaagri.gov.in
