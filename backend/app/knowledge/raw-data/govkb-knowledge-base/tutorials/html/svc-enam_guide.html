eNAM (National Agriculture Market) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://enam.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Online trading platform integrating APMC mandis to create a unified national market for agricultural commodities.

Eligibility

Farmers, traders, and FPOs registered with linked APMC mandis.

Benefits

Better price discovery, transparent bidding, wider buyer reach, reduced intermediaries.

Documents

  • Bank account (Mandatory)
  • Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Trader licence (for traders) (Mandatory)
  • GST (traders if applicable) (Mandatory)
  • FPO registration (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Register on enam.gov.in as farmer/trader.
  2. Step 2: Link APMC mandi and bank account.
  3. Step 3: List produce with quality/grade.
  4. Step 4: Participate in e-auction; buyer bids.
  5. Step 5: Payment settled to farmer account post-trade.

Fees

Transaction charges as per APMC bye-laws; platform access free for farmers.

Timeline

Trade settlement as per mandi rules (typically within mandated days).
