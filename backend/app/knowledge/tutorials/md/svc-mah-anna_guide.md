# Maha Anna (Maharashtra Ration / NFSA) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://mahafood.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Maharashtra NFSA ration card and Public Distribution System for subsidised foodgrains.

## Eligibility
Maharashtra households meeting NFSA/state poverty criteria.

## Benefits
Subsidised rice/wheat/cereals via Fair Price Shops; portability under One Nation One Ration Card.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Income proof (Mandatory)
- Family details (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Apply via MahaFood portal / rationing office.
2. Verification by authority.
3. Issuance; avail PDS.
