# SWAYAM (Free Online Courses) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://swayam.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

National MOOC platform offering free online courses from school to postgraduate level with certificates.

## Eligibility

Open to all learners; some courses have eligibility for credit/ exam.

## Benefits

Free quality courses, certificates (with exam fee), academic credit transfer where recognised.

## Documents Required

- **Email/mobile for sign-up** (Mandatory)
- **Institution enrollment for credit** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Sign up on swayam.gov.in.
**Step 2:** Browse and enrol in courses.
**Step 3:** Attend video lectures, assignments.
**Step 4:** Take proctored exam (optional, paid) for certificate.

## Fees

Course free; certificate exam fee applicable.

## Timeline

Self-paced; exam per schedule.
