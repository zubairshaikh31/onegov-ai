GST Registration & Returns (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.gst.gov.in.

Eligibility

Businesses with aggregate turnover above threshold (Rs. 20 lakh/10 lakh/40 lakh by category) or mandated (inter-state, e-commerce, casual).

Benefits

Legal tax identity, input tax credit, interstate trade, compliance certificates.

Documents

  • PAN
  • Aadhaar (verification)
  • Business registration
  • Address proof
  • Bank account
  • Photograph
  • Authorisation letter

Process

  1. Apply Part-A (Form GST REG-01) on gst.gov.in.
  2. Get ARN; complete Part-B with documents.
  3. Aadhaar authentication / verification.
  4. GSTIN allotted on approval.
  5. File returns (GSTR-1/3B) periodically.

Official: https://www.gst.gov.in
