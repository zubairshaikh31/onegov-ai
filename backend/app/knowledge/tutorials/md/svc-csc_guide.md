# Common Service Centres (CSC) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.csc.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
CSC network provides public services (gov, finance, education) at village/urban kiosks.

## Eligibility
Village-level entrepreneurs and citizens seeking assisted services.

## Benefits
Access to govt services, banking, insurance, tele-law at local kiosk.

## Documents
- Aadhaar
- Address proof

## Process
1. Locate nearest CSC.
2. Request service with Aadhaar.
3. Complete assisted application.
