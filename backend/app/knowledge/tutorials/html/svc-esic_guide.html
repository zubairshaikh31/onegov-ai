ESIC (Employees' State Insurance) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.esic.gov.in.

Eligibility

Employees with wage up to Rs. 21,000/month (Rs. 25,000 for persons with disability) in ESIC-covered factories/establishments.

Benefits

Cashless medical care for worker and family, sickness/maternity benefits, disability and dependent benefits.

Documents

  • IP number / UAN
  • ESIC card / Pehchan card
  • Aadhaar
  • Bank account
  • Employer certificate

Process

  1. Employer registers establishment and enrols employees (Form 1).
  2. Employee gets Insurance/Pehchan card.
  3. Avail treatment at ESIC dispensary/hospital with card.
  4. File claims (sickness/maternity) online if applicable.

Official: https://www.esic.gov.in
