# PAN Card (Permanent Account Number) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.incometax.gov.in. Last updated 2025-01-01._

## Introduction
PAN is a 10-character alphanumeric identifier issued for all tax-related and high-value financial transactions.

## Eligibility
Individuals, companies, HUFs, and entities carrying out financial transactions in India.

## Benefits
Mandatory for filing income tax returns, opening bank accounts, and high-value transactions.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (for instant e-PAN) (Mandatory)
- Proof of Identity (Mandatory)
- Proof of DOB (Mandatory)
- Proof of Address (for corrections) (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Visit the Income Tax e-filing portal.
2. Select 'Instant e-PAN' (Aadhaar-based) or apply via NSDL/UTIITSL.
3. Enter Aadhaar number and validate OTP.
4. Confirm details and submit.
5. Download e-PAN in PDF format.

## Fees
Instant e-PAN free; regular PAN Rs.93 (Indian address) + GST.

## Timeline
e-PAN via Aadhaar: instant to 10 minutes; physical card: 15 days.
