Digital Gujarat Portal (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://digitalgujarat.gov.in.

Eligibility

Gujarat residents per service criteria.

Benefits

Single-window certificates, scholarships, and schemes.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income/caste proof

Process

  1. Register on digitalgujarat.gov.in.
  2. Select service; apply.
  3. Track and download.
