eNAM (National Agriculture Market) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://enam.gov.in.

Eligibility

Farmers, traders, and FPOs registered with linked APMC mandis.

Benefits

Better price discovery, transparent bidding, wider buyer reach, reduced intermediaries.

Documents

  • Bank account
  • Aadhaar
  • Trader licence (for traders)
  • GST (traders if applicable)
  • FPO registration

Process

  1. Register on enam.gov.in as farmer/trader.
  2. Link APMC mandi and bank account.
  3. List produce with quality/grade.
  4. Participate in e-auction; buyer bids.
  5. Payment settled to farmer account post-trade.

Official: https://enam.gov.in
