UMANG (Unified Mobile App for New-age Governance) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://umang.gov.in.

Eligibility

Any citizen with a smartphone; services may need respective IDs.

Benefits

One app for many government services; multilingual; Aadhaar/PAN-based auth.

Documents

  • Mobile number
  • Service-specific IDs (Aadhaar/PAN/UAN)
  • Bank details for payments

Process

  1. Download UMANG app / visit web.umang.gov.in.
  2. Register with mobile OTP.
  3. Browse departments and services.
  4. Authenticate per service and transact.

Official: https://umang.gov.in
