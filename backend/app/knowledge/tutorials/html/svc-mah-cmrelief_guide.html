Maharashtra Chief Minister Relief Fund (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.maharashtra.gov.in.

Eligibility

Maharashtra residents in notified distress/calamity situations.

Benefits

Financial assistance for calamity, medical, and ex-gratia support.

Documents

  • Identity proof
  • Distress/calamity proof
  • Bank account

Process

  1. Apply through district administration / online portal.
  2. Verification of distress.
  3. Disbursement of relief.

Official: https://www.maharashtra.gov.in
