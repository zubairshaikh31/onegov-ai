Domicile / Residence Certificate (Maharashtra) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in.

Eligibility

Persons residing in Maharashtra for the qualifying period as per rules.

Benefits

Required for education admissions, government jobs and domicile-based benefits.

Documents

  • Aadhaar / ID proof
  • Address proof
  • Proof of residence period
  • School leaving certificate
  • Birth certificate
  • Affidavit

Process

  1. Login to Aaple Sarkar portal.
  2. Select Revenue Department > Age Nationality and Domicile Certificate.
  3. Fill the form and upload documents.
  4. Pay fees and submit.
  5. Download the digitally signed certificate.

Official: https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
