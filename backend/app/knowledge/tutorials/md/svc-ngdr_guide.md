# NGDRBharat (Digitized Land Records) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.ngdrbharat.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
National Generic Document Registration System providing unified digital land/property registration across states.

## Eligibility
State residents using integrated land registration offices.

## Benefits
Transparent, faster property registration; digital records; reduced fraud.

## Documents
- Sale deed (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Party identity proof (Mandatory)
- Encumbrance certificate (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Visit state NGDRBharat/Registration portal.
2. Fill deed details and upload documents.
3. Pay stamp duty; book appointment; sign digitally.
