# GeM (Government e-Marketplace) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://gem.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Online platform for procurement of goods and services by government buyers and selling by sellers/MSMEs.

## Eligibility
Sellers (including MSMEs) and government buyers; sellers need business registration, bank, tax details.

## Benefits
Transparent public procurement, direct market access, MSE preference, timely payment.

## Documents
- Business registration / Udyam (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)
- PAN/GSTIN (Mandatory)
- Product catalogue (Mandatory)
- Brand authorization (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Register as buyer or seller on gem.gov.in.
2. Complete organization and bank/KYC.
3. List products/services (sellers) or create demand (buyers).
4. Transact via bid/RA; order and fulfil.

## Fees
No fee for buyers; sellers pay one-time caution money + per-transaction charges per GeM policy.

## Timeline
Registration quick; transaction per bid cycle.
