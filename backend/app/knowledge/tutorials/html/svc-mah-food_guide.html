Maharashtra Ration Card / Food Supply (MahaFood) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://mahafood.gov.in.

Eligibility

Maharashtra households meeting NFSA/state poverty criteria.

Benefits

Subsidised foodgrains via PDS; priority/APL/BPL categories.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income proof
  • Family details
  • Earlier ration card

Process

  1. Apply via Maharashtra food supply portal / CSC.
  2. Submit family and Aadhaar details.
  3. Verification by rationing authority.
  4. Ration card issued; avail PDS.

Official: https://mahafood.gov.in
