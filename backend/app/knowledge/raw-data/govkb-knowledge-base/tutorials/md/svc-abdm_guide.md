# Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABHA) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://abdm.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Creates a digital health ecosystem: ABHA ID, Health ID, Personal Health Records (PHR), and interoperable health information exchange.

## Eligibility

Any Indian resident (voluntary opt-in).

## Benefits

Single health ID to link records across providers; consent-based sharing; easier continuity of care.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar / Driving Licence / any govt ID for ABHA creation** (Mandatory)
- **Mobile number for OTP** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Visit abdm.gov.in or ABHA app.
**Step 2:** Authenticate with Aadhaar OTP or other ID.
**Step 3:** Create 14-digit ABHA number / Health ID.
**Step 4:** Link PHR address and records from providers.
**Step 5:** Manage consent for record sharing.

## Fees

Free.

## Timeline

Instant ABHA creation.
