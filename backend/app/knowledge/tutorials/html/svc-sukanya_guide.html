Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.indiapost.gov.in.

Eligibility

Parents/guardians of a girl child below age 10; one account per child (max 2 per family).

Benefits

High interest (govt-set), tax-free, partial withdrawal for education/marriage at 18+.

Documents

  • Birth certificate of girl child
  • Parent guardian ID
  • Address proof
  • Photograph

Process

  1. Open at post office or authorised bank.
  2. Deposit yearly (min Rs. 250).
  3. Manage until maturity at age 21.

Official: https://www.indiapost.gov.in
