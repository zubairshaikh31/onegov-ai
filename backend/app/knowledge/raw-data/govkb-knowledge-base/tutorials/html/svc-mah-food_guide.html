Maharashtra Ration Card / Food Supply (MahaFood) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://mahafood.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Maharashtra ration card issuance and PDS entitlements under NFSA/state schemes.

Eligibility

Maharashtra households meeting NFSA/state poverty criteria.

Benefits

Subsidised foodgrains via PDS; priority/APL/BPL categories.

Documents

  • Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Address proof (Mandatory)
  • Income proof (Mandatory)
  • Family details (Mandatory)
  • Earlier ration card (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Apply via Maharashtra food supply portal / CSC.
  2. Step 2: Submit family and Aadhaar details.
  3. Step 3: Verification by rationing authority.
  4. Step 4: Ration card issued; avail PDS.

Fees

As per state schedule (nominal).

Timeline

Per district verification (weeks).
