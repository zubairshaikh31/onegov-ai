EPF / UAN Services (Employees' Provident Fund) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://www.epfindia.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Mandatory provident fund, pension (EPS), and insurance (EDLI) for organised-sector employees; managed via UAN.

Eligibility

Employees with basic wage up to statutory limit in establishments covered under EPF Act.

Benefits

Retirement corpus with interest, pension, withdrawal/advance facilities, transfer on job change.

Documents

  • UAN (Mandatory)
  • Aadhaar (seeded) (Mandatory)
  • Bank account (Mandatory)
  • PAN (for certain withdrawals) (Mandatory)
  • Form 15G/15H (Optional)
  • Employer certificate (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Activate UAN on EPFO member portal / UMANG.
  2. Step 2: Seed Aadhaar, bank, PAN via KYC.
  3. Step 3: Raise claim (Form 19/10C/31) online.
  4. Step 4: Track claim status; amount credited to bank.

Fees

No fee; statutory contributions by employer/employee.

Timeline

Claims typically 7-20 days after verification.
