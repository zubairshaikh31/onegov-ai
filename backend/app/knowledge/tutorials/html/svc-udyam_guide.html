Udyam Registration (MSME) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://udyamregistration.gov.in.

Eligibility

Enterprises meeting MSME investment/turnover thresholds (manufacturing/services).

Benefits

Priority credit, subsidies, tender preferences, easier compliance, scheme eligibility.

Documents

  • Aadhaar of proprietor/partner/director
  • PAN & GSTIN (if applicable)
  • Business bank account
  • Investment/turnover details
  • MSME previous registration (EM-II/UAM) for migration

Process

  1. Visit udyamregistration.gov.in; Aadhaar-validate.
  2. Enter PAN and business details.
  3. Fill investment/turnover and activity.
  4. Submit; download Udyam certificate instantly.

Official: https://udyamregistration.gov.in
