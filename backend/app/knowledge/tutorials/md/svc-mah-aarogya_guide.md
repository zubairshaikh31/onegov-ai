# Mahatma Jyotiba Phule Jan Arogya Yojana (MH) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.jeevandayee.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Maharashtra cashless health scheme for specified families.

## Eligibility
Maharashtra families (yellow/orange ration card / eligible).

## Benefits
Cashless treatment at empanelled hospitals.

## Documents
- Ration card
- Aadhaar

## Process
1. Check eligibility.
2. Visit empanelled hospital.
3. Avail cashless treatment.
