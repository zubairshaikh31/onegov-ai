Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (Urban) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://pmaymis.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Credit-linked subsidy and affordable housing support for urban poor/EWS/LIG/MIG to own a pucca house.

Eligibility

Urban households in EWS/LIG/MIG categories with no pucca house; income and property criteria apply.

Benefits

Interest subsidy on home loan (CLSS) and support for house construction.

Documents

  • Aadhaar (Mandatory)
  • Income proof (Mandatory)
  • Address proof (Mandatory)
  • Bank account (Mandatory)
  • Property/land documents (Mandatory)
  • Caste/category certificate (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit pmaymis.gov.in; check eligibility.
  2. Step 2: Apply under beneficiary-led / CLSS component.
  3. Step 3: Enter Aadhaar, income, bank, property details.
  4. Step 4: Verification by ULB/state.
  5. Step 5: Subsidy/assistance as per component.

Fees

Free to apply.

Timeline

Verification and sanction per state/ULB cycle.
