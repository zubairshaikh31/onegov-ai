# DigiLocker — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://www.digilocker.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Digital locker to store and share verified electronic documents issued by government agencies; reduce physical paperwork.

## Eligibility

Indian citizens with Aadhaar (used for sign-up).

## Benefits

Instant access to issued documents (marksheets, licences), verified e-documents, paperless verification.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar (for sign-up/authentication)** (Mandatory)
- **Mobile number** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Sign up at digilocker.gov.in with Aadhaar OTP.
**Step 2:** Link mobile; set username/password.
**Step 3:** Fetch issued documents from registered issuers.
**Step 4:** Upload personal documents; share via URI/QR.

## Fees

Free.

## Timeline

Instant; document fetch depends on issuer.
