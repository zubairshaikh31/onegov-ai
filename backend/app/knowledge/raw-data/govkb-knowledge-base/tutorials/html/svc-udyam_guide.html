Udyam Registration (MSME) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

Based on verified official information from https://udyamregistration.gov.in. Verify on the official portal.

Introduction

Online registration of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises to obtain a Udyam certificate and access schemes/incentives.

Eligibility

Enterprises meeting MSME investment/turnover thresholds (manufacturing/services).

Benefits

Priority credit, subsidies, tender preferences, easier compliance, scheme eligibility.

Documents

  • Aadhaar of proprietor/partner/director (Mandatory)
  • PAN & GSTIN (if applicable) (Mandatory)
  • Business bank account (Mandatory)
  • Investment/turnover details (Mandatory)
  • MSME previous registration (EM-II/UAM) for migration (Optional)

Application Process

  1. Step 1: Visit udyamregistration.gov.in; Aadhaar-validate.
  2. Step 2: Enter PAN and business details.
  3. Step 3: Fill investment/turnover and activity.
  4. Step 4: Submit; download Udyam certificate instantly.

Fees

Free (no government fee).

Timeline

Instant certificate on submission.
