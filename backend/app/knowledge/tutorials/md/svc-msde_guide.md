# Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.msde.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Flagship skill-certification scheme offering free short-term training and certification to youth.

## Eligibility
Indian youth (often 15-45) seeking skill certification.

## Benefits
Free training, certification, placement support, monetary reward on certification.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Educational proof (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)
- Age proof (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Find PMKVY training centre (Training Partner).
2. Enrol with Aadhaar and documents.
3. Complete training; appear for assessment; get certificate.
