# National Scholarship Portal (NSP) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://scholarships.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Centralised portal for central/state/UT scholarship schemes with application, verification, and DBT.

## Eligibility

Students meeting scheme-specific criteria (merit, income, category, institution).

## Benefits

Single-window scholarships; direct benefit transfer to bank account.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar** (Mandatory)
- **Institution ID** (Mandatory)
- **Income certificate** (Mandatory)
- **Bank account** (Mandatory)
- **Previous marksheets** (Mandatory)
- **Caste/disability certificate** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Register on scholarships.gov.in as student.
**Step 2:** Find eligible schemes; fill application.
**Step 3:** Upload documents; submit.
**Step 4:** Institute and nodal verification.
**Step 5:** Sanction and DBT to bank.

## Fees

Free.

## Timeline

Per scheme cycle (application->verification->sanction).
