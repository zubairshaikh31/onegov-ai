Passport Issuance (Passport Seva) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://passportindia.gov.in.

Eligibility

Indian citizen; must provide proof of date of birth, address, and nationality. Minors require parent consent.

Benefits

Internationally recognised travel document; enables visa-free/visa-on-arrival access; Tatkaal for urgent issue.

Documents

  • Proof of Date of Birth
  • Proof of Address (current)
  • Proof of nationality / old passport (if renewing)
  • Annexure as applicable
  • Marriage certificate (name change)
  • ECNR eligibility proof

Process

  1. Register on passportindia.gov.in and fill the application form.
  2. Pay fee and book appointment at nearest PSK/PopSK.
  3. Visit PSK with originals + photocopies for document verification and biometric capture.
  4. Police verification (for fresh passports) at registered address.
  5. Passport printed and dispatched; track status online.

Official: https://passportindia.gov.in
