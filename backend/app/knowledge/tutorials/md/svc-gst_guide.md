# GST Registration & Returns — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.gst.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Goods and Services Tax registration, return filing, and compliance for businesses via the GST portal.

## Eligibility
Businesses with aggregate turnover above threshold (Rs. 20 lakh/10 lakh/40 lakh by category) or mandated (inter-state, e-commerce, casual).

## Benefits
Legal tax identity, input tax credit, interstate trade, compliance certificates.

## Documents
- PAN (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (verification) (Mandatory)
- Business registration (Mandatory)
- Address proof (Mandatory)
- Bank account (Mandatory)
- Photograph (Mandatory)
- Authorisation letter (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Apply Part-A (Form GST REG-01) on gst.gov.in.
2. Get ARN; complete Part-B with documents.
3. Aadhaar authentication / verification.
4. GSTIN allotted on approval.
5. File returns (GSTR-1/3B) periodically.

## Fees
No government registration fee; late fees for delayed returns.

## Timeline
Registration usually within 7-30 days; returns monthly/quarterly.
