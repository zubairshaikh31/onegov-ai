# NIELIT Certification & Services — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.nielit.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
NIELIT provides electronics/IT training, certifications (O/A/B/C level), and digital literacy courses.

## Eligibility
Candidates per course (schooling/diploma as applicable).

## Benefits
Govt-recognized IT/electronics certifications; employment readiness.

## Documents
- Educational certificates (Mandatory)
- Aadhaar (Mandatory)
- Photo (Mandatory)

## Application Process
1. Choose course on nielit.gov.in.
2. Apply online / at centre.
3. Complete training and examination; get certificate.
