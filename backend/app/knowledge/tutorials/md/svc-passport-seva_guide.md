# Passport Seva (Re-enrich) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://www.passportindia.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
Passport issuance, renewal, miscellaneous services.

## Eligibility
Indian citizens.

## Benefits
Passport for travel.

## Documents
- Birth cert
- Address proof
- Aadhaar

## Process
1. Register on Passport Seva.
2. Pay & book appointment.
3. Visit PSK; police verify; receive passport.
