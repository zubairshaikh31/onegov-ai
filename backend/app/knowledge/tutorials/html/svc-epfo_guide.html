EPF / UAN Services (Employees' Provident Fund) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://www.epfindia.gov.in.

Eligibility

Employees with basic wage up to statutory limit in establishments covered under EPF Act.

Benefits

Retirement corpus with interest, pension, withdrawal/advance facilities, transfer on job change.

Documents

  • UAN
  • Aadhaar (seeded)
  • Bank account
  • PAN (for certain withdrawals)
  • Form 15G/15H
  • Employer certificate

Process

  1. Activate UAN on EPFO member portal / UMANG.
  2. Seed Aadhaar, bank, PAN via KYC.
  3. Raise claim (Form 19/10C/31) online.
  4. Track claim status; amount credited to bank.

Official: https://www.epfindia.gov.in
