Income Certificate (Maharashtra) (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in.

Eligibility

Residents of Maharashtra requiring proof of family income.

Benefits

Required for scholarships, fee concessions, reservations and welfare schemes.

Documents

  • Aadhaar / ID proof
  • Address proof
  • Income proof (salary slip/self-declaration)
  • Ration card
  • Talathi report
  • Affidavit

Process

  1. Register/login on Aaple Sarkar (RTS) portal.
  2. Select Revenue Department > Income Certificate.
  3. Fill applicant details and upload documents.
  4. Pay the fee online.
  5. Track application; download the digitally signed certificate.

Official: https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in
