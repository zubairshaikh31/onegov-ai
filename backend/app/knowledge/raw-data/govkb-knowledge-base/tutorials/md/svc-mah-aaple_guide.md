# Aaple Sarkar / MahaOnline Citizen Services — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _This guide is an unofficial explanatory summary based on verified official information from https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in. Always verify on the official portal._

## Introduction

Maharashtra's integrated online portal (Aaple Sarkar / MahaOnline) for certificates, schemes, and citizen services.

## Eligibility

Residents of Maharashtra; service-specific criteria.

## Benefits

Single window for caste/income/residence certificates, scheme applications, grievance redressal.

## Documents Required

- **Aadhaar** (Mandatory)
- **Address proof** (Mandatory)
- **Service-specific documents** (Mandatory)
- **Earlier certificates** (Optional)

## Application Process

**Step 1:** Register on Aaple Sarkar portal.
**Step 2:** Select department/service (certificate/scheme).
**Step 3:** Fill form and upload documents.
**Step 4:** Submit; track application.
**Step 5:** Collect certificate (online download / SETU centre).

## Fees

As per state fee schedule per service.

## Timeline

Per service SLA (certificates usually 7-30 days).
