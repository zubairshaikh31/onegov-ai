# Parivahan (Vahan & Sarathi) — Citizen Guide (Unofficial Explanatory)

> _Compiled from verified official information from https://parivahan.gov.in. Last updated 2024-01-01._

## Introduction
National transport portal for vehicle registration (Vahan) and driving licence/learner licence (Sarathi) services.

## Eligibility
Vehicle owners and driving licence applicants as per Motor Vehicles Act.

## Benefits
Online RC, DL, permits, tax, slots; reduces RTO visits.

## Documents
- Aadhaar (where enabled) (Mandatory)
- Address/age proof (Mandatory)
- Vehicle invoice/insurance (RC) (Mandatory)
- Passport photo (DL) (Mandatory)
- Medical certificate (age/transport) (Optional)

## Application Process
1. Visit parivahan.gov.in -> Vahan/Sarathi.
2. Apply for RC/DL/LL with details and documents.
3. Pay fees and book RTO slot.
4. Visit RTO for test/verification.
5. Receive RC/DL (digital via DigiLocker).

## Fees
As per state MV fee schedule; varies by service.

## Timeline
RC/DL issued after RTO test/verification (days to weeks).
