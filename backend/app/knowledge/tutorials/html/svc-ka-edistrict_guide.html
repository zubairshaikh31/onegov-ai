Karnataka Seva Sindhu / e-District (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in.

Eligibility

Karnataka residents per service criteria.

Benefits

Online certificates, ration, and welfare schemes.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income proof

Process

  1. Register on sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in.
  2. Apply; upload docs.
  3. Track and download.

Official: https://sevasindhu.karnataka.gov.in
