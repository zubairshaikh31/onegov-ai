Uttar Pradesh e-District / Ration (Unofficial Guide)

Based on verified official info from https://edistrict.up.gov.in.

Eligibility

Uttar Pradesh residents per service criteria.

Benefits

Online certificates and scheme applications.

Documents

  • Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Income proof

Process

  1. Register on edistrict.up.gov.in.
  2. Apply and upload documents.
  3. Verify and collect certificate.

Official: https://edistrict.up.gov.in
